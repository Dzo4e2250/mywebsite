Sales as psychology

I don't sell. I listen, understand, connect.

Philosophy: Sales isn't pushing a product. It's understanding the person in front of you - what they need, what worries them, what they're missing.

How this looks in practice:

  • 👂 First I listen: Before I say anything, I understand the situation. The customer tells you what they need - if you give them space.
  • 🚪 I open doors, not force: A product or service can start a conversation, not generic pushing.
  • 🎯 I solve problems: I don't sell features. I sell solutions for specific problems the customer has.
  • 🤝 I build trust: If I show up repeatedly, I build a relationship - even if we don't say much.

Result: When you listen and the customer becomes an "open book", you get information for the right solution. Sales becomes a conversation, not a battle.
